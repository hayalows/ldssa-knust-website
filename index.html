Who We Are

A Community Built on Faith & Service

LDSSA (Latter-day Saint Student Association) at KNUST brings together students seeking spiritual growth and meaningful friendships on campus.

Spiritual Growth

Weekly devotionals, scripture study groups, and spiritual development activities to strengthen your faith journey.

Community

Build lasting friendships with fellow students who share your values in a welcoming, inclusive environment.

Service

Participate in community service projects that make a real difference in the lives of people around KNUST campus.

Leadership

Develop leadership skills through organizing events, mentorship programs, and student-led initiatives.
